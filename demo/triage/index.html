About this model

For: helping people sort a flood of disaster posts. Not for: deciding who gets help.

How it works. Stage 1 scores how likely a post is to be informative (p_informative). Posts with p_informative ≥ τ go to Stage 2, which picks one of 10 humanitarian categories, or Other for an informative post that fits none of them (p_category is its score for that category). The rest go to Filtered out. Nothing is deleted, so a missed call for help stays one click away.

Known limitations

  • English only.
  • Trained on CrisisBench tweets from about 2010–2018, before Twitter became X. How people post has changed since.
  • It only sees people who post. People who lost power or signal, don't use X, or don't write in English are missing, and the loudest posters aren't always the most urgent.
  • Scores aren't certainty. Posts near τ or with a low category score are marked needs review.
